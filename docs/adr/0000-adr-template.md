## Options considered

For each viable option record:

- description;
- advantages and disadvantages;
- security, tenant, privacy, cost, data, and operational impact;
- reversibility and migration;
- evidence and unresolved uncertainty.

## Decision

State one unambiguous decision and its boundary. List what is explicitly not decided. A Proposed ADR does not authorise implementation beyond the current gate.

## Consequences

Record positive, negative, new risks, operating burden, lock-in, cost, and future constraints.

## Implementation and migration

List affected contracts, modules, data/migrations, configuration, flags, observability, documentation, rollout, rollback/roll-forward, and removal of superseded paths.

## Verification

List exact unit, contract, integration, architecture, security, migration/recovery, and user-journey evidence. Include negative/adversarial cases and the no-live-provider mode where relevant.
